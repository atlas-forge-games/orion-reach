Orion Reach v0.54.0 - Diplomacy statistics

INSTALL
Requires the existing v0.53.1 game. This is an incremental update.
Close the game, extract this ZIP into the repository root (the directory
containing main.py), and allow the included files to overwrite their older
versions. Restart with python main.py.

CHANGES
- Open Diplomacy and select Statistics beside Rival Report.
- Six graph tabs track population, industry, food production, mining
  production, energy generation, and credits per turn across your empire.
- Your own civilization is always visible. Other civilizations appear when
  diplomatic contact is established, revealing their full recorded history.
- New campaigns record every civilization from turn 1. Civilizations founded
  later have no history before they exist. Earlier turns remain fixed; the
  current turn's point updates as orders and worker assignments change.
- Hover over the graph to see exact totals for a turn. Click a civilization
  in the legend to show or hide its line. Choose All turns, Last 25, or Last
  100 to change the displayed range. Negative credit income is supported.
- Population includes enslaved inhabitants. Industry is production available
  for construction and Trade Goods. Food and mining show gross production
  before consumption and power-plant fuel use. Energy shows actual generation
  across local grids. Credits show net income after maintenance, including
  Trade Goods and diplomatic trade income.
- Observer reports also provide Statistics for all civilizations.

COMPATIBILITY
Save format is now 38, with per-turn statistics retained across save/load.
Existing campaigns load normally and start recording from their current
turn: earlier versions did not save economic history, so earlier values
cannot be reconstructed. The graph leaves that period blank and explains
when records began. Keep v0.54.0 or newer to open format-38 saves.
No additional dependencies are required. Save files and local settings
are not included in this update.
