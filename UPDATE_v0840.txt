AGE OF THE STARS / ORION REACH — v0.84.0 DEBT INTEREST AND BANKRUPTCY

INSTALL
Close the game. Extract this ZIP over v0.83.4 into the folder containing
main.py, orion/ and assets/. Replace the included files, then run python main.py.
This is an incremental update, not a complete game.

DEBT RULES
- Negative credits incur 10% interest each completed turn, rounded up.
- Interest uses the opening negative balance and is charged once.
- Net income in the interface already includes interest.
- At -10,000 CR or lower you lose through bankruptcy, even with all optional
  victory conditions disabled. A projected warning appears before this happens.
- AI empires pay the same interest and are eliminated if they go bankrupt.
- The Observer has no economy and cannot personally go bankrupt.

SAVES
Save format 60 supports bankruptcy. Older saves load without being rewritten.
An ongoing save already at or below -10,000 CR becomes bankrupt on load when
no combat is pending, or after the pending battle resolves. Completed old
campaign outcomes are preserved. No campaign restart or new dependency is needed.

VALIDATION
All 15 new debt and bankruptcy tests pass. Of 39 additional economy and victory
checks, 33 pass and six fail identically on untouched v0.83.4. Details are in
docs/treasury.md. The budget, warning and defeat captures were inspected.
