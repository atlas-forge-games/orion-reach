Orion Reach v0.51.1 - Empire stockpile display

Requires the v0.51.0 construction queue and Trade Goods update.

INSTALL
Extract this ZIP into your existing Orion Reach folder (the folder containing
main.py). Allow the included files to be replaced, then restart the game.
Keep all other files. Existing saves work unchanged.

CHANGES
- Compact Empire stockpile strip at the top of the planet overview and
  construction screen.
- Shows total Credits, Resources and stored Research plus empire-wide rates.
- Credit income includes upkeep and Trade Goods. Resource income is net of fuel.
- Values refresh immediately after orders, purchases and worker changes.
- Debt and negative income are highlighted.
- Keeps colony production and the Observer header separate.
