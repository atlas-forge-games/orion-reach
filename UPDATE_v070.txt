Orion Reach v0.70.0 — standalone combat simulator

Install over v0.69.1: close the game, extract this ZIP into the folder
containing main.py, and replace matching files.

Start from the same Python/Conda environment used for the game:

    python combat_simulator.py

Configure your fleet and the enemy fleet, then fight manually or watch
AI vs AI. The simulator shares the game's actual combat engine, animation,
ship designer, weapons, shields and carrier/drone mechanics.

Features:
- Standard ships and custom loadouts; all hulls and equipment available.
- Independent quantities, race and combat research for both sides.
- Up to 60 ships and three planet-bound missile bases per side.
- Six ready-to-play presets, plus a fleet-copy button.
- First-move selection, deterministic seed and normal/guaranteed-hit modes.
- A to take control, P/Esc to pause, and 1x/4x/12x playback speed.
- R to replay the original matchup with fully restored fleets.
- F5 saves named scenarios and custom designs; Load scenario restores them.
- Optional --balance, --scenario, --preset, --seed and --data-dir arguments.

Simulation never loads or writes campaign saves or advances strategic turns.
Scenario files save the initial setup, not an in-progress battle. They use
the currently loaded balance profile. Ordinary saves remain compatible:
save format 50 and balance profile 14 are unchanged.

See docs/combat-simulator.md for the full guide. The discussed race-specific
victory ideas are preserved in docs/race-victory-proposals.md as future
design notes; they are not implemented by this update.
