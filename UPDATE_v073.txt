ORION REACH v0.73.0 — TACTICAL APPROACH AND SHIP INSPECTION

INSTALL
Close the game. Extract this ZIP into your existing v0.72.2 game folder (the
folder containing main.py), merging folders and replacing the listed files.
This is an incremental update; the earlier game files and assets are required.
It includes all changes since v0.72.2. No files need deleting.

Start the simulator:
    python combat_simulator.py

WHAT CHANGED
- Fleets begin 17 cells apart on a 24-column battlefield. Thirteen rows fit
  onscreen; large fleets scroll. Relative ship sizes remain distinct.
- Torpedoes and missiles are visible homing projectiles traveling three cells
  per round. A stationary target eight cells away takes three advances to
  reach. Each side gets a response turn before incoming shots can first hit.
- Aim and rolls lock at launch. Current shields, point defense and escorts
  apply on impact; moving changes the path and ETA. Six advances of fuel.
- Missiles keep flying after their launcher dies while combat continues.
  Dead targets, retreat and battle resolution clear the relevant flights.
- Stationary planetary batteries deploy ahead of fleets and remain in range
  of opposing batteries; their surface launch and impact effects remain.
- Right-click either fleet's ship in the simulator for condition, shields,
  defenses, weapons, equipment and incoming/outgoing ordnance with ETAs.
  The panel pauses manual/automatic combat and animations. Esc/Close resumes
  the same selection and control mode. Larger loadouts have pages.
- The campaign and simulator share these combat rules. Individual ship
  orders remain in use; there are no selectable battle plans.

SAVES
Campaign format is now 52. In-flight projectiles and deterministic hit rolls
are saved. Older saves remain readable and keep their existing tactical
positions; older versions already applied missile damage at launch, so their
saves migrate with an empty flight list. New battles use wider deployment.
Simulator scenario format remains unchanged (initial setups, not active saves).

VERIFICATION
The full 1,280-test regression run completed. Its twelve legacy expectations

all affected tests pass on rerun (42 designer/hull/victory tests and 34 guardian
tests). The final simulator/projectile/support/planetary suite passed 60 tests.
The earlier focused combat run also passed 140 tests. Rendered screenshots
were visually checked, including enemy inspection and projectile travel.
Expected malformed-image/audio fixture diagnostics can appear during tests.
