Orion Reach v0.65.2 — extract over v0.65.1, then restart the game.

Performance improvements are enabled automatically:
- System visibility uses direct local checks instead of scanning the galaxy.
- Each redraw reuses fleet visibility, system intelligence and supply queries.
  Results are discarded after drawing, so new orders and turns remain current.
- Routine mouse, scroll and typing events are batched. Click targets are rebuilt
  before subsequent actions, including screen changes and camera movement.
- A Processing turn panel appears before simulation starts. Rapid duplicate
  turn presses and commands entered while busy are ignored. The finished turn
  is displayed before accepting another fresh press. Production prompts cannot
  be bypassed by duplicate turn commands in the same input batch.

Gameplay rules, AI decisions and normal visual effects are preserved.
Autosave remains independently configurable in Settings. Save format 46 and
balance profile 11 are unchanged, and existing campaigns load directly.

See docs/performance.md for measurements and verification. Run
python tools/benchmark_performance.py to measure the same temporary scenario
on your machine. The benchmark does not modify your campaign saves.
