Orion Reach v0.65.0 — extract over v0.64.1, then restart the game.

Wanderers can research Forgotten hyperlanes from the solar-system overview or
the galaxy sidebar. Each system project costs 300 RP and discovers a random
permanent connection to another ordinary star system. The destination is only
revealed on completion. This does not occupy the megastructure slot.

Station a mothership, then allocate 0–100% of local research or invest banked RP.
Multiple stationary homes pool their research. These RP are deducted from normal
technology research, not created for free. Departing or pausing retains progress.

Only the discovering empire can use its routes. Ships at either endpoint travel
to the other in one turn, including motherships and escorts, without fuel limits.
The route remains after the homes leave. Guardians and hostile fleets still
intercept arrivals. AI Wanderers can research and use their own routes.

Includes original portal artwork, clickable system symbols, galaxy connections,
one-turn course previews, discovery news and encyclopedia guidance.

Existing saves load directly. Save format is now 46; older source saves are not
rewritten on loading. Balance profile 11 remains compatible. Nothing committed.
Detailed rules: docs/forgotten-hyperlanes.md.
