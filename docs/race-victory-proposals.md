# Race-specific victory concepts

Design archive from v0.70. The playable v0.72 implementation is described in
[civilization-legacies.md](civilization-legacies.md). Some larger quest and cargo
mechanics below remain future ideas.

| Civilization | Proposed goal | Core idea |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Serevan Union | Many Peoples, One Future | Voluntary membership from several cultures, meaningful surviving populations and a stable constitutional convention that fulfills member promises. |
| Dominion of One | The Last Independent Thought | Assimilate different civilizations' major population centres and mental patterns, then sustain an Overmind synchronization broadcast. Liberation severs its connections. |
| Skirr Swarm | The Great Hatching | Feed and mature queen broods across separated regions, then protect their synchronized hatching. Destroying nests or their food supplies interrupts it. |
| Terrans | The Galactic Charter | Build a federation of independent empires through joint undertakings, dispute resolution and security guarantees, then ratify its charter. |
| Kharuun | The Eternal Work | Assemble a planet-sized crystalline monument using specialized production sites and vulnerable construction convoys. |
| Aelari | The Living Archive | Preserve a complete record of civilizations, ecosystems, ancient history and cosmic phenomena through expeditions, agreements and rescued archives. |
| Lithar | Awaken the Mountains | Seed consciousness into hostile planets through deep excavation, geological projects and long settlement, uniting planetary minds in the Continuum. |
| Varkesh | The Throne of Fear | Carry unique trophies home from rival capitals and sustain tribute long enough to unite the clans at a Great Moot. Trophy ships can be intercepted. |
| Xenonflora | The Second Spring | Restore worlds and plant World Trees, including through beneficial agreements with foreign rulers, then synchronize a galaxy-spanning living biosphere. |
| Nexus | The Final Computation | Dedicate machine citizens to connected, star-powered processing cores and defend the network through its final computation. |
| Aetheri | The Star That Woke | Send populations on cosmic pilgrimages, gather different resonances and protect their Convergence to awaken a conscious star. |
| The Remnant | The Throne Beyond Memory | Recover star-chart fragments, locate a lost imperial capital and restore the First Throne using ancestral memories from tomb worlds. |

Future design rules:

- Scale requirements to galaxy size and eligible generated content.
- Avoid impossible goals when civilizations are absent or extinct; ruins,
  records or alternative acquisition paths may be needed.
- Give rivals observable milestones and meaningful ways to interrupt the
  final stage. Do not silently change the existing Wanderer victory.
- Objectives can show known empires' aggregate progress without exposing
  undiscovered names, coordinates or sites.
- Keep the existing race-specific victory toggle and progress visibility
  rules. Exact thresholds, costs, mechanics and AI plans still need design.
