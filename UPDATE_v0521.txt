Orion Reach v0.52.1 - research pacing and available construction

INSTALL
Requires v0.52.0. Close the game/editor, extract this ZIP into your existing
Orion Reach folder (beside main.py), and replace the matching files.
Start normally with: python main.py
This is an incremental update, not a standalone game.

CHANGES
- Standard research prices multiplied by four at every tier.
  Tiers I-VIII: 160, 400, 480, 880, 1440, 2160, 3040, 4160 RP.
  Ascension Theory: 20000 RP.
- Planet construction catalogues show only eligible projects. Locked,
  completed, queued and slot-blocked buildings are hidden immediately.
- All buildings are one per planet, counting both completed buildings and
  queued orders, including paused queues. This applies to players and AI.
- Cancelling an order or demolishing the building permits building it again.
  Ships remain repeatable. Trade Goods remains available.
- Resources are still reserved when work starts; orders can wait for supplies.
- AI development, farming and power choices respect the same building limit.

SAVES AND EDITOR PROFILES
Existing v0.52 saved campaigns and custom balance.json profiles retain their
research prices. The higher standard prices apply to new default campaigns.
To adjust an existing editor profile, open Research costs and enter the values
above. Individual technology cost overrides continue to take precedence.
Existing buildings and queued work are preserved, including older duplicates;
no new duplicate orders can be placed. Save format 36 remains compatible.
No dependencies or artwork are added. Nothing has been committed or pushed.
