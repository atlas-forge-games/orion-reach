AGE OF THE STARS / ORION REACH — v0.83 SIX RESEARCH FIELDS

Extract over v0.82 into the repository folder containing main.py and orion/.
Replace the included files, then launch normally with python main.py.
Repository, package, launcher and save-directory names are unchanged.

Start a NEW CAMPAIGN to use the new default research catalogue:
  Energy technology, Weapons technology, Spaceflight,
  Industry, Biotechnology, Xenoscience.

Every tier has THREE alternatives. Research ONE to permanently exclude the
other two and open the next tier. Any earlier choice works; there are no
connected research paths. Unfinished projects retain the existing free switch
and RP-transfer rules. Research Arrays retain their rare recovery exception.

Economic research now unlocks separately constructed facilities. Existing
factories, labs and other buildings do not automatically improve. New facilities
consume resources, construction work, a colony slot, power and upkeep. Population
facilities support biological growth, assembly and passive growth. Environmental
adaptation preserves racial preferences and the actual planet type. Gas giants
remain uncolonizable. Six new high-resolution illustrations accompany the update.

Right-click technologies for both alternatives and full unlock details. The
colony catalogue has page controls; the balance editor supports three slots.
AI empires use the same rules and pursue range technology when expansion stalls.

COMPATIBILITY
Existing campaigns keep their original tree, completed choices, bonuses, costs
and active project. Custom balance.json files also retain their old tree. To
adopt the six-field defaults, use Reset all in the balance editor, save a separate
profile, and start a new campaign with that profile. Keep your original profile
if you want to continue using its custom values.

New saves use format 59; balance profiles use version 21. Update all installations
before sharing these saves. See docs/six-field-research.md for all 33 tiers,
facilities and validation results.
