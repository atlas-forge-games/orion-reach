AGE OF THE STARS / ORION REACH — v0.87.0 CONSISTENT NAVIGATION

INSTALL
Close the game. Extract this ZIP over v0.86.0 into the folder containing
main.py, orion/ and assets/. Replace the included files, then run python main.py.
This is an incremental update, not a complete game. No new dependency is needed.

CHANGES
- Galaxy keeps its existing End turn button.
- Every other campaign screen has Galaxy map and End turn together at the
  bottom right, in that order, using one shared navigation component.
- G opens the galaxy map; E advances the turn. Escape keeps its local return.
- System and Colony links remain close to their own content.
- Footer actions, pagination and planet details make room for the shared pair.
- Required battles, transmissions, votes and reports still block campaign
  navigation. Hover over a dimmed button to see the reason.
- The selected system and planet, colony production housekeeping and normal
  turn prompts are preserved. Combat retains its separate End combat turn.

VALIDATION
All 27 campaign screens checked; 68 screen/dialog states rendered at three
window/zoom combinations. Ten focused navigation tests pass. Broader checks and
existing baseline failures are recorded in docs/ui-navigation.md.
Save format remains 60; simulation and balance rules are unchanged.

No files were committed or pushed by this update.
