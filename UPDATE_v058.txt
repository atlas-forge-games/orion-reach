Orion Reach v0.58.0 — specialist portraits and planetary outposts

INSTALL
Extract this incremental archive over the v0.57 project folder. Preserve the
folder structure and replace files when asked. Start with python main.py.
No new dependency is required. Your saves and balance.json are not overwritten.

CHANGES
- Mara Voss and Orun Basalt have portraits in the specialist roster, details,
  and their assigned planet overview.
- Standard colony ship cost: 800 Resources / 420 work (both increased 10x).
- Outpost ship cost: 80 Resources / 42 work, the previous colony-ship price.
- Send an outpost ship into orbit, select an uncolonized planet, then choose
  Build outpost. Any biome works. All races, including Varkesh, can use them.
- One outpost per solar system across all empires. The ship is consumed.
- Bases extend fuel range and provide live local observations. They have no
  population, production or upkeep and are not colonies for victory or leaders.
- An outpost is a visible unarmed surface installation with 30 hull. After
  clearing orbital defenders, armed ships can Attack outpost from its planet
  screen. Each ship shares its normal once-per-turn bombardment allowance.
  Attacks end treaties and set relations to -100. Destruction removes supply
  coverage immediately. Peaceful bases repair 4 hull/turn after a quiet turn.
- An owned outpost can be replaced with a normal colony using a colony ship.
  Normal habitability requirements apply. A defeated empire loses its bases;
  a Remnant awakening on the same planet replaces the unarmed outpost.
- The AI builds/deploys useful outposts, attacks hostile bases, saves materials
  for the larger colony-ship investment and favors known new systems.
- Fuel coverage is one uniformly transparent blue union, with only its boundary
  drawn. Overlapping internal circles disappear. Gaps remain outside supply.

SAVES AND BALANCING
Save format 40; balance profile version 5. Old campaigns remain loadable. Their
former standard colony costs migrate from 80/42 to 800/420. Intentional custom
cost values are preserved. Queued orders retain their reserved material costs;
work requirements use the active ship definition. Starting fleets are unchanged.
Ship costs and Global rules > Outposts are editable through python editor.py.
