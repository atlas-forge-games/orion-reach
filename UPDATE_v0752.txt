ORION REACH v0.75.2 — SHIP DETAIL AND VISIBLE ORDNANCE

INSTALL
Close the game. Extract this ZIP over your installed v0.75.1 game, into the
folder containing main.py. Merge folders and replace the included files.
Keep the new orion/projectile_art.py module with the updated combat files.
This incremental update uses your existing full-resolution ship PNGs.
No dependency or save-format change is required.

CHANGES
- Ships scale directly from the original high-resolution illustrations.
  The old small-icon enlargement step caused the blur at high combat zoom.
- Procedural guardians and missing-art fallbacks render at larger sizes too.
- Torpedoes have armoured bodies and coloured warheads. Defense missiles
  have pointed bodies and fins. Both have exhaust and face their flight path.
- Warheads remain visible at their current positions between turns.
  During movement they follow the interpolated flight path and remain at
  the endpoint while the rest of the salvo animation finishes.
- Impacted or fuel-expired projectiles disappear after their final segment.
  Launch flashes no longer show a second projectile ahead of its real position.

VALIDATION
55 targeted tests passed in 5.700 seconds with Python 3.12.14 and
pygame-ce 2.5.8 using dummy SDL display/audio drivers. The checks cover
real launch/advance/impact rendering, projectile persistence, expiry,
rendering without state changes, zoom, tactical orders, planetary batteries,
sound cues and existing support presentation. Full-suite discovery was not run.

Command:
PYTHONPATH=tests SDL_VIDEODRIVER=dummy SDL_AUDIODRIVER=dummy PYGAME_HIDE_SUPPORT_PROMPT=1 python3 -m unittest test_projectile_art test_combat_zoom test_combat_projectiles test_tactical_combat test_combat_planets test_combat_sounds test_fleet_support.SupportPresentationTests -q

Real simulator screenshots at 200% and 300% zoom were visually inspected.
