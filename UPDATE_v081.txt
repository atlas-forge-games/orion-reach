AGE OF THE STARS / ORION REACH — v0.81 GAS GIANT ORBITAL STATIONS

Install this incremental update over v0.80:
1. Close the game.
2. Extract this ZIP into the repository folder containing main.py and orion/.
3. Replace the included files. Keep existing assets, saves and other files.
4. Launch normally: python main.py

Open a gas giant's planet overview. Choose Gas refinery, Energy platform or
Research station. A local colony or anchored mothership provides crews;
otherwise an idle outpost ship is consumed. Listed costs are additional.
Assembly takes two turns. Upkeep starts immediately.

One station fits each gas giant. Planets have deterministic atmospheric
properties that modify costs or output. Energy is shared locally across owned
colonies; refinery Credits and station research feed the empire budget.
Stations can be blockaded, bombarded, captured with a troop transport after
being damaged, or dismantled. AI empires use the same rules.

Gas giants are still not colonizable. Aetheri atmospheric habitats are deferred.
Stations do not extend ship range and do not count as colonies.

Existing campaigns load without changing their planets. Save format is now 57;
balance profiles remain version 20. Repository and launch filenames are unchanged.
