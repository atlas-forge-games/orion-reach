ORION REACH v0.78 — SPECIES ENVIRONMENTS AND TOXIC WORLDS

Install over your existing v0.77 game folder:
1. Close the game.
2. Extract this ZIP into the project root, where orion/ and assets/ already are.
3. Allow the included files to replace their previous versions.
4. Start the game normally. Start a new campaign to get the new native homes
   and naturally generated Toxic worlds.

Included changes
- Growth preferences for all 13 species across all eight planet types.
- Terrans: 100% on Terran, 50% on Ice, 25% on Toxic.
- Nexus: 60% on Terran, 150% on Ice, 110% on Toxic.
- Skirr: 80% on Terran, 40% on Ice, 150% on Toxic.
- The multiplier applies to food-driven growth, assembly and passive growth.
- Mixed colonies use a population-weighted factor from their actual residents.
- Mobile habitats retain 100% environmental growth efficiency.
- Toxic planets have a detailed new landscape, globe and procedural fallback.
- A full Skirr population can use toxic biota for six natural food per colony.
- New Skirr homes are Toxic, Nexus homes Ice and Aelari homes Ocean.
- AI planet choices consider the species' preference, richness and world size.
- Race selection, setup, planet/colony views and the encyclopedia show the rules.
- All growth preferences and the toxic-food bonus are editable in balance settings.

Compatibility
- Existing saves keep their current planets, homeworlds and populations.
- The new multipliers apply to their future growth after loading, so older
  campaigns can have different growth estimates and economic timing.
- Older custom balance profiles retain their previous settings and gain the
  new defaults. Loading does not rewrite the source save or balance file.
- Save format is 55; balance profile format is 19.
- Adding a new rival initializes its native terrain and updates charted terrain
  without revealing stale ownership or population changes.
- Existing food, capacity, racial settlement and technology rules still apply.
- Custom balance.json files, saved games and settings are not in this ZIP.

Details and validation
- docs/planet-environments.md contains the full preference matrix and formulas.
- docs/planet-environment-validation.md records checks and baseline limitations.
- tools/preview_environments.py renders the new screens for local review.
- This package includes only the new Toxic PNG; the seven v0.77 landscapes and
  the original building sprites must already be installed.

This is an incremental update, not a complete game distribution.
