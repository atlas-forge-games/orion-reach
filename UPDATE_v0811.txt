AGE OF THE STARS / ORION REACH — v0.81.1 BUILDING INFORMATION

Extract over v0.81 into the repository folder containing main.py and orion/.
Replace the included files, then launch normally with python main.py.

Right-click a building in the planetary overview to see what it does:
- Purpose and base effects at that planet's mineral richness.
- Workforce bonuses, base maintenance and energy demand.
- Operating, disabled, unpowered or queued status.

Also supports headquarters, shipyards and mothership modules. Empty module
bays do not open construction on right-click. Old surveys show only information
that was previously observed, without exposing current enemy conditions.

Close with Esc, another right-click or Close. Left-click behavior is unchanged.
This is an information-only UI change. Save format remains 57 and balance
profiles remain version 20. No new artwork or dependencies are required.
