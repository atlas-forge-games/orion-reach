ORION REACH v0.74.0 — AUTOMATIC FLEET FLAGSHIPS

INSTALL
Close the game. Extract this ZIP into your existing v0.73.1 game folder (the
folder containing main.py), merging folders and replacing the listed files.
This is an incremental update; previous game files and assets are required.
No files need deleting. Run python main.py or python combat_simulator.py.

AUTOMATIC COMMAND
Each fleet automatically chooses its strongest surviving crewed ship. Armed
ships and carriers take priority over unarmed civilians. Within that group,
the rating is maximum hull + maximum shields + 4 x volley damage + 12 x drone
bays, with the lower ship ID breaking ties. Current damage, shield depletion,
cooldowns and expended drone launches do not cause command to jump around.

New battles put the flagship at the centre of its fleet's starting formation,
including fleets spanning several deployment columns. Planets keep a separate
defensive line. A small flag, close halo and faint empire-coloured coverage
circle identify the flagship. The aura follows movement and combat zoom.

The flagship gives OTHER crewed ships in its own fleet +8 accuracy and +5
evasion percentage points within a hull-dependent command radius:
    Scout / frigate:                  2 cells
    Destroyer / civilian transports: 3 cells
    Cruiser:                          4 cells
    Carrier:                          5 cells
    Battleship:                       6 cells
    Titan / mothership:               8 cells
Colony ships and outpost ships also use the 3-cell civilian radius.
Distance is straight-line distance between cell centres, including the edge.
Drones, planetary batteries and space monsters do not hold or receive fleet
command. Pirate ships and civilian convoys can have flagships.

Only the highest command accuracy bonus applies. A stock titan's flag bridge
still gives +12 aim within 4 cells; its innate flagship aura gives +8 farther
out, up to 8 cells. Fleet evasion combines with normal evasion and ECM, subject
to normal accuracy caps. Every shot hits disables aim/evasion/ECM effects.
No additional equipment, actions, battle plans or manual selection is needed.

When a flagship is destroyed or disabled, the next strongest survivor takes
command immediately, using its own hull radius and current position. Nearby
bonuses change with movement and casualties. The AI lets its flagship lead;
manual ship orders remain available as before. The sidebar identifies friendly
and enemy flagships, and the simulator inspector explains status and coverage.

SAVES AND EXISTING FEATURES
Save format remains 52 and scenario format remains 1. Existing battles retain
their positions; flagships and bonuses are derived from their surviving ships.
Torpedo rolls remain locked at launch, including command modifiers. Loaded
warheads are not rerolled. Five-cell projectile travel, delayed impacts, zoom,
right-click inspection and existing fleet-support modules remain in place.
