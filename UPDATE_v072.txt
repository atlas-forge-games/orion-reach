Orion Reach v0.72.0 - Civilization-specific victories
18 October 2026

INSTALL
Extract this incremental update over your complete v0.71.1 game folder, next
to main.py. Replace the included files. No files need deleting.
Start with python main.py as before.

WHAT CHANGED
All thirteen civilizations have a race-specific victory goal. The twelve
planetary civilizations unlock their own installation after eight technologies,
build through ordinary colony production and sustain a five-turn finale.
Wanderers retain their unique forgotten-hyperlane victory.

Terran: The Galactic Charter - independent friendships and lasting peace.
Kharuun: The Eternal Work - fund a monument through industrial foundries.
Aelari: The Living Archive - personal exploration, diverse biomes and research.
Lithar: Awaken the Mountains - mature minds on harsh worlds.
Varkesh: The Throne of Fear - raid trophies, a strong fleet and the Great Moot.
Xenonflora: The Second Spring - mature World Trees across different biomes.
Nexus: The Final Computation - machine citizens, scientists and dedicated RP.
Aetheri: The Star That Woke - stellar exploration and different resonances.
Dominion of One: The Last Independent Thought - founding worlds and assimilation.
Serevan Union: Many Peoples, One Future - willing cultures and peaceful membership.
Skirr Swarm: The Great Hatching - spread and mature well-fed Queen Nests.
Remnant: The Throne Beyond Memory - ancient sites and memory reconstruction.
Wanderers: Recover every forgotten hyperlane - independently map the lost network.

Open Objectives > Your race objectives for exact targets, live progress,
installation status and known rivals. AI empires use the same costs and checks.
Blockades, bombardment, starvation and power loss can interrupt operation.
Captured installations are destroyed. Simultaneous race completions draw.
Requirements adapt to galaxy size, available content and solo campaigns.
See docs/civilization-legacies.md for the complete implemented rules.

ASCENSION RETIREMENT AND SAVE MIGRATION
Ascension Theory and gate construction are retired from active campaigns.
Conquest and Galactic Council victory remain available.

Save format is 51 and balance profile format is 16. Ongoing old Science games
switch to Race-specific. Invested Ascension RP and paid gate materials are
refunded under the campaign's loaded balance profile. Retired gates and orders
are removed, including stale survey records. Completed science results remain
historical snapshots that can be viewed and saved again.

Other technology choices, fleets, colonies, combat and Wanderer discoveries
retain their normal migrations. New legacy history starts at migration; old
raids, membership consent and site maturity are not invented retrospectively.
Existing custom balance values are preserved and new buildings gain defaults.
