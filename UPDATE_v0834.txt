AGE OF THE STARS / ORION REACH — v0.83.4 ROUTE PREVIEWS AND QUEUED FLEETS

INSTALL
Close the game. Extract this ZIP over v0.83.3 into the folder containing
main.py, orion/ and assets/. Replace the included files, then run python main.py.
This is an incremental update, not a complete game.

IN CLEAN MAP MODE
- Select a fleet and hover over a star to preview a dashed route.
- Gold means reachable; red indicates an out-of-range destination.
- Click the destination and the icon immediately lines up on the route,
  facing its target. There is no need to wait until the next turn to see this.
- Ships left behind remain docked in their existing positions.
- Issued routes stay visible after closing the fleet panel and saving/loading.
- Hovering another target previews a redirect; clicking it changes the order.
- Click the origin before End Turn to cancel and return to the dock.

The visual placement does not advance time or spend movement. Travel still
advances with End Turn. Classic, campaign rules, settings and save format are
unchanged. No new dependency or campaign restart is needed.
