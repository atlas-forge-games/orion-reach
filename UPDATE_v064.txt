Orion Reach v0.64.0 — extract over v0.63.0, then restart the game.

Wanderers now live aboard giant motherships instead of planets.
Each home supports 50 B population and 12 facilities, with its own workforce,
food, growth and production. New campaigns start with one mothership and two scouts.

Galaxy-map habitat-ring markers show population and escorts or arrival time.
Click the fleet, then Home to manage the habitat. Empire and Fleets also open it.
Set course uses the existing fleet controls. Motherships and selected escorts
travel at one quarter of normal speed. Supply, sensors and trade distances follow
the moving home. Its empire remains alive while travelling.

Onboard income, research, resources and facility construction continue in flight.
Ship construction and gate charging wait until arrival. New motherships cost
1200 Resources and 600 work before the Wanderer ship discount, include initial
settlers, and have 180 unarmed hull. AI Wanderers build and relocate their homes.
Destroying a mothership loses its habitat; losing the last one eliminates the empire.
Wanderers cannot colonize or occupy planets. Planetary defenses stay planet-only.

Save format 45 converts old Wanderer colonies and colony ships, preserving their
population and compatible facilities/work. Planet-only buildings and incompatible
queued projects are refunded. Active battles finish before conversion.
Balance profile 11 adds the mothership and preserves other settings.

See docs/wanderer-motherships.md for details. Nothing committed.
