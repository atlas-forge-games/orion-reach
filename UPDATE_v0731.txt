ORION REACH v0.73.1 — COMBAT ZOOM AND FASTER TORPEDOES

INSTALL
Close the game. Extract this ZIP into the existing v0.73.0 game folder (the
folder containing main.py), merging folders and replacing the listed files.
This is an incremental update and requires the previous game files/assets.
No files need deleting. Start with python main.py or python combat_simulator.py.

TORPEDO SPEED
Torpedoes and missiles now travel five cells per round. A stationary target
eight cells away is reached after two advances. The enemy still gets a turn
to respond; shields and escorts apply on arrival. Existing hit rolls, launch
ranges, weapon cooldowns and six-advance fuel limits are unchanged.
Five cells is faster than most warships, but scouts, drones, diagonal movement
and some thruster-equipped designs can match or exceed it.

COMBAT CAMERA — CAMPAIGN AND SIMULATOR
- Mouse wheel over the battlefield: zoom around the cursor (50–300%).
- + / minus keys or buttons: zoom around the view center.
- Middle mouse drag: pan in any direction.
- Shift + arrow keys or bottom arrow buttons: pan.
- Shift + wheel or Page Up / Page Down: scroll vertically.
- Horizontal wheel: scroll horizontally.
- Percentage button, 0 or Home: return to 100% and reveal the selected ship.
- N: select and reveal the next ready ship, horizontally and vertically.
The sidebar stays fixed. Ships, movement cells, weapons, planets, projectiles,
support ranges and simulator inspection share the same camera. Zoom/pan spend
no combat actions and do not change ranges or time. Click targets are clipped
to the battlefield, and stale targets are cleared when the camera changes.
Right-click inspection pauses as before and works at every magnification.

SAVES
Save format remains 52. Loaded projectiles retain their positions, fuel age and
locked rolls, and use the new five-cell speed on their next advance. The camera
is presentation-only; new battles, loaded campaigns and rematches start at 100%.
Scenario file format remains unchanged.
