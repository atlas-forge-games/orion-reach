Orion Reach v0.62.0 — extract over v0.61.0.

Diplomacy now has a clickable portrait strip with each contacted empire's signed
relation score. Gifts and envoys have been removed.

Trade is accepted at +10 by every race and adds +1 relations per active turn.
Non-aggression is accepted at +0, prevents attacks/raids/bombardment and adds
+1 relations per active turn. Friendship is accepted at +30 and includes trade,
non-aggression and bilateral live scanner sharing. Research remains separate.

Friendship preserves each source scanner's detail level. It shares direct fleet
detections, not planet surveys or friends-of-friends' intelligence. Ending it
removes the bundled treaties and stops live sharing immediately; dated sightings
remain. Declare war ends all agreements and sets relations to -100.

Trade income per partner:
round((0.25 * sqrt(own gross colony CR * partner gross colony CR) + own bonuses)
      / (1 + distance between capitals in light-years / 50)).
Gross colony credits include Trade Goods, before upkeep; treaty income and
treasury reserves do not count. Values recalculate as the economies change.

Save format 43 reads previous versions. Existing relation scores, trade/research
pacts and logs are preserved. New treaties start inactive. Balance profiles
upgrade to version 9 with new treaty and trade controls; retired envoy/gift and
flat-trade controls are removed. Campaigns keep their other balance settings.

Nothing committed.
