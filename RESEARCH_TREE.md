# Standard research tree — v0.56

Each tier offers two exclusive choices. Complete one choice to open the next tier in that branch. Later facility types do not require the earlier facility to be built or researched.

New campaigns use this layout unless a custom balance profile is configured. Existing campaigns retain their saved tree.

## Economic facility progression

Output below is before research and racial bonuses, at average mineral richness. Miner bonuses and fixed mineral extraction scale with richness. All fixed outputs and workforce bonuses stack when the buildings are powered; maintenance is paid even when disabled or unpowered.

| Facility | Research unlock | Fixed output | Workforce bonus | Resources | Work | Energy | CR upkeep |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Factory | Available at start | 4 industry | +1 / worker | 45 | 24 | 3 | 2 |
| Robotic factory | Planetary Automation (Computers III) | 8 industry | +2 / worker | 110 | 66 | 6 | 4 |
| Nanofactory | Nanostructured Materials (Materials & Chemistry VII) | 14 industry | +3 / worker | 230 | 138 | 10 | 7 |
| Mining complex | Available at start | 2 resources | +1 / miner | 30 | 18 | 2 | 1 |
| Deep-core mine | Deep Core Drills (Materials & Chemistry III) | 4 resources | +2 / miner | 90 | 54 | 5 | 3 |
| Automated refinery | Automated Refineries (Materials & Chemistry V) | 7 resources | +3 / miner | 180 | 108 | 8 | 5 |
| Research lab | Available at start | 4 RP | +1 / scientist | 50 | 30 | 4 | 2 |
| Advanced research lab | Advanced Laboratories (Physics IV) | 8 RP | +2 / scientist | 120 | 72 | 7 | 4 |
| Quantum research lab | Quantum Research Archives (Computers VII) | 14 RP | +3 / scientist | 240 | 144 | 11 | 7 |

Each distinct building can be constructed once per planet and occupies one slot. Advanced buildings are optional additional facilities, so smaller worlds can prioritize one advanced facility instead of filling every level of a family.

Factory bonuses apply to factories, robotic factories and nanofactories. Laboratory bonuses apply to all three laboratory types. Mine research bonuses apply once per operational mine/refinery, outside mineral-richness scaling.
