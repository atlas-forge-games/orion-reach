# Resource illustrations

Original science-fiction HUD illustrations generated for Orion Reach on
2026-10-10 with the built-in image-generation tool:

- `credits-v1.png`: credit discs and gold ingots.
- `research-v1.png`: scientific instruments and an illuminated atom.
- `food-v1.png`: a colony supply crate containing grain and fresh produce.

The unmodified generated PNGs are scaled by the game at runtime. No artwork
from Master of Orion or other games is included. The exact prompts are recorded
in `generation-prompts.json`.
