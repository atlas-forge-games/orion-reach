# Age of the Stars title artwork

These three PNGs implement the approved logo and title-screen concept:

- `background-v1.png`: clean planetary and nebula background, without text or ships.
- `logo-v1.png`: celestial emblem and AGE OF THE STARS wordmark, with alpha transparency.
- `fleet-v1.png`: three exploration ships and engine trails, with alpha transparency.

All three were derived with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool from the
approved conversation concepts. The original generated bytes and alpha channels
are preserved. `generation-prompts.json` records the exact prompts and reference
roles; `manifest.json` records dimensions, modes and SHA-256 checksums.

The menu labels and buttons are drawn by pygame in `orion/title_screen.py`.
They are not baked into the artwork. Source and display surfaces are cached;
scaling preserves aspect ratio. The background covers the game's 1440x900
canvas, with any overflow cropped at the right edge. Ships drift independently
of the stationary logo and controls. The animation setting also stops sparkles.

The existing project asset license applies. The Atlas Forge studio logo and
intro remain separate and use their existing files.
