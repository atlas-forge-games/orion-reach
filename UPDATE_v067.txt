Orion Reach v0.67.0 — extract over v0.66.1, then restart the game.

Race-specific victory:
- A fourth independent new-game toggle enables race-specific victories.
- Wanderers win by discovering every unique forgotten hyperlane themselves.
  Other races display "No race-specific goal defined yet".
- Objectives → Race-specific goals shows your count and progress, plus portraits,
  goals, counts, percentages and progress bars for empires you have met.
  Unknown empires and hidden route locations are not disclosed.
- Turning the condition off preserves research, travel and progress tracking.
- Rivals can also win. Simultaneous final discoveries share the win.

Forgotten hyperlanes:
- All hidden connections are fixed when the galaxy is created.
- Approximately 50% of ordinary systems have at least one route, 25% at least
  two, 12.5% at least three, and so on. Black holes are excluded.
- A system can have zero or several routes. Research costs 300 RP for each,
  with one unfinished project per empire per system. Research another route
  appears after a completed discovery if more connections remain.
- Each two-way connection counts once, regardless of which end discovers it.
- Only Wanderers can discover and use routes. Every empire researches its own
  private access; another empire's discoveries do not count as yours.
- AI Wanderers research local routes, then explore reachable unvisited systems
  when pursuing this victory. Remote hidden endpoints are not used to guide them.

Compatibility:
- Save format 48 preserves existing routes, projects and hyperlane voyages.
- Older saves receive a stable hidden network containing their existing routes,
  while retaining their original victory settings. Start a new campaign to
  enable the new victory. Loading does not rewrite the original save file.
- Balance profile 12 is unchanged.
