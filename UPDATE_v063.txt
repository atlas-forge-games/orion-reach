Open Diplomacy → Coalitions & tribute.

Coalitions target a named empire. Shared wars, known territorial pressure,
racial interests, relationships, cooperation and betrayal determine acceptance.
Members have mutual non-aggression and +1 relations per turn. Target aggression
calls coalition partners into defence. Offensive wars do not automatically enlist
partners. Joining an existing war explicitly requires confirmation. Several
bilateral agreements can create a larger coalition. No scanner sharing is bundled.

Fear is a separate 0–100 assessment using observed military forces, proximity,
recent defeats, allied protection and racial resolve. Hidden fleets and worlds
do not contribute; the diplomacy dialog explains the calculation.

Demand money tribute (three turns of gross income, capped by available treasury)
or, for Varkesh, one population unit as slaves. AI empires accept at fear 75.
One demand uses a 100-turn cooldown across every target and both types, including
refusals. Demands harm relations and encourage protection/coalitions, but do not
automatically declare war. Human recipients explicitly accept or refuse.
Population tribute cannot empty a colony and respects existing slave capacity.

All eleven racial priorities guide treaty choices and coalition goals. Lithar
retain betrayal memory permanently. Trade remains available at +10. AI empires
form their own treaties and coalitions and can demand tribute. New decisions
appear in Turn news. There is no resource or technology trading; access and
mediation are preferences, not new standalone diplomatic actions.

Save format 44 loads older saves without resetting relations or existing treaties.
Balance profile 10 adds racial priorities and coalition/tribute settings while
retaining other values. Pending tribute responses and cooldowns survive reload.
