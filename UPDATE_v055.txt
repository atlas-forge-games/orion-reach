Orion Reach v0.55.0 - Galaxy map zoom and panning

INSTALL
Requires the existing v0.54.0 game. This is an incremental update.
Close the game, extract this ZIP into the repository root (the directory
containing main.py), and allow the included files to overwrite their older
versions. Restart with python main.py.

CONTROLS
- Mouse wheel over the map: zoom from 100% to 400% around the cursor.
- Map minus/plus buttons or keyboard + / minus: zoom around the map centre.
- Hold right or middle mouse button and drag: pan in any direction.
- Hold Shift + arrow keys: continuous panning.
- Horizontal mouse wheel / trackpad: pan sideways.
- Shift + mouse wheel: pan vertically.
- Fit button, 0 or Home: restore the full galaxy.
- Center button or C: bring the selected system back into view.

BEHAVIOR
Stars, moving fleets, courses, sensors, fuel-range overlays and the background
move together. Names and fleet badges retain their readable size. Offscreen
objects have no stray labels or click targets along the map edges. The view
stays within the galaxy bounds, including when zooming out after panning.

Camera controls also work while selecting a fleet destination. Dragging does
not issue orders or cancel selected ships: left-click a destination as usual.
The wheel over the side panel still pages through planets or the manifest.
Ordinary arrow-key navigation is retained, and browsing systems brings the
selected system into view. Mouse input respects resized/letterboxed windows.

The camera survives visits to other screens and resets when starting or
loading a campaign. Losing focus, opening a dialog or leaving the map stops
dragging and held-key panning. Observer campaigns use the same controls.

COMPATIBILITY
Save format remains 38. Existing campaigns load normally. This is a display
update; it does not change population, race balance, discoveries or game rules.
The discussed balancing and deep-investigation ideas remain future proposals.
No additional dependencies are required. Save files and local settings are
not included in this update.
