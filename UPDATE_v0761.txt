Orion Reach v0.76.1 — cleaner galaxy map

Install on top of v0.76:
1. Close the game.
2. Extract this ZIP into the folder containing main.py, orion/ and assets/.
3. Replace the included files, then run python main.py normally.

This is an incremental update, not a full game. Existing campaigns use the
new presentation immediately; no new campaign or dependency is required.

Undiscovered systems no longer show map labels such as UNK-012 or their label
connector lines. Their star markers remain clickable. Names appear after
exploration, and stored survey names remain visible. Disabling fog of war or
using observer mode continues to show known names. Charted black holes retain
their detected names. Fleet and name placement no longer reserves space for
unexplored labels.

The background keeps the blue and copper nebula composition, with prominent
decorative stars and diffraction spikes removed. Fine, dim texture and diffuse
distant galaxies remain. The active asset is assets/backgrounds/galaxy-v2.png;
the original galaxy-v1.png is preserved. The background setting still works.
The built-in image edit mode and exact prompt are documented in
assets/backgrounds/README.md.
