Orion Reach v0.60 — system megastructures

Extract this update into your v0.59 project, replacing files when asked.
Run python main.py as usual. No new dependencies are required.

Open a solar system and select Megastructures. Four staged projects are available:

Dyson Sphere: Power & Propulsion tier 4. Four stages cost 20,000 RES / 4,000 work.
Early stages add 30 energy per owned colony each. Completion supplies UNLIMITED
energy empire-wide. Enabled buildings and gates remain powered without fuel.

Stellar Forge: Construction tier 3. Total 18,000 RES / 3,500 work.
Ship production in its system is 4x / 7x / 10x / 13x as stages complete.
Buildings, Trade Goods and other industry uses receive no boost. Ships still
require their normal material costs and researched designs.

Research Array: Physics tier 7. Total 15,000 RES / 3,000 work.
1% / 2% / 3% / 4% chance each production turn to discover one unknown technology,
including previously omitted choices or higher tiers. No normal RP output.
If it discovers the active research or its alternative, invested RP is refunded.
Ascension Theory can be found only when science victory is enabled.

Asteroid Mining Network: Materials & Chemistry tier 6. Total 16,000 RES / 3,200 work.
150 / 300 / 450 / 600 resources per turn directly into the empire reserve.
This is orbital output, unaffected by planetary richness or administrative strain.

Each civilization may commission each type once per campaign, even if it is
later lost or destroyed. Only one structure may occupy each solar system.
Only the sole settled civilization may start, repair or advance construction.
Rival outposts do not count as settlements. Shared settlement or hostile armed
ships pause work without losing progress. Completed sections continue operating
while their owner retains a colony and there is no hostile armed blockade.

Choose 0 / 25 / 50 / 75 / 100% local industry, or pause construction. Costs are
paid progressively; only funded industry is diverted. Fuel is funded first,
followed by megastructure work and ordinary colony queues. Stage costs increase
1x / 2x / 3x / 4x; newly finished stages yield benefits on the following turn.
There is no recurring megastructure upkeep in this first version.

Structures have hull and can be attacked after orbital defenses are defeated.
Attacks use ships' existing once-per-turn bombardment action. Repairs take
priority over construction and require industry plus resources. Losing every
colony can transfer a structure to a sole new settler, with construction paused.
Captured duplicate structures do not stack effects. Last-seen surveys preserve
old structure information without exposing current activity.

AI civilizations obey the same construction and uniqueness rules and invest
only with substantial resource reserves. The balance editor includes all four
specifications, output rates, discovery chance, costs, hull and repair costs.

Save format 42 / balance format 7. Existing saves and profiles are upgraded on
load. Recovered research choices remain valid across saves. Earlier versions
cannot load the new format; keep a backup before returning to an older release.
