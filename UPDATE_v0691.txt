Orion Reach v0.69.1 — undiscovered system names

Install over v0.69.0: close the game, extract into the folder containing main.py,
and replace matching files.

Unexplored stars use neutral catalogue IDs such as UNK-012. The real name
appears after exploration and remains known after leaving the system.
First contact and remote scans do not reveal undiscovered home system names.

Covered surfaces: galaxy labels, system panels, destination previews, fleet
lists, mothership courses, notifications, fuel errors, sensor reports, hyperlane
endpoints and retreat destinations.

Fog-off and Observer campaigns retain full names. Detected black holes keep
their existing names. Actual system names and galaxy generation are unchanged.
Works with existing saves; save format 50 and balance profile 14 are unchanged.
