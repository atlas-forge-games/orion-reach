Orion Reach v0.53.1 — Portraits and ship designer planning

INSTALL
Requires the existing v0.53.0 game. This is an incremental update, not a
standalone game. Close the game, extract this ZIP into the repository root
(the directory containing main.py), and allow the included files to overwrite
their older versions. Restart with python main.py.

CHANGES
- Aelari, Lithar and Remnant use the supplied new portraits. The PNGs are
  preserved byte-for-byte under the existing asset filenames. Race selection,
  diplomacy and first contact all use the replacement art after restarting.
- Right-click any equipment row in the ship designer, including basic laser
  cannons, to see fitting costs, actual hull-scaled statistics, range, accuracy,
  tracking, special effects and research requirements. The arrows page through
  longer details. Escape, Close or another right-click dismisses the popup.
- Plan, edit, auto-fit and save blueprints with unresearched hulls and modules.
  Adding and removing modules both work before research. Physical fitting
  rules remain in force: slot counts, one scanner, carrier-only bays and the
  two-bay maximum.
- Planned designs display their missing research. View research requirements
  lists every missing hull/module technology and indicates excluded choices.
  Auto-fit previews the chosen hull using known modules and its native
  equipment; other future modules can be selected manually.
- Construction requires every hull and equipment technology, through the
  designer, shipyard and colony production. A saved design becomes buildable
  automatically after its requirements are discovered; no resave is needed.
- Saved future plans survive loading. Existing ships and paid orders retain
  their equipment, even when their library blueprint is revised into a plan.
  AI construction continues to use only researched hulls and equipment.

COMPATIBILITY
Save format stays at 37; this version loads existing campaigns. The update
adds no dependencies and does not change research costs or campaign balance.
Keep the new game files when loading campaigns that contain future plans.
Your save files, balance.json and local settings are not included or replaced.
