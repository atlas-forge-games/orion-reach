Orion Reach v0.53.0 - Construction columns and hull-sized modules

INSTALL
Requires the existing v0.52.1 game. This ZIP is an incremental update, not a
standalone game. Close the game and editor, extract into the folder containing
main.py, and replace the included files. Run python main.py as usual.
No new dependencies or assets are needed. Start the editor with python editor.py.

CONSTRUCTION
Buildings are on the left, the construction queue is in the middle, and ships
are on the right. Each catalogue has its own pages. Queue multiple projects,
cancel them or change their priority without leaving the screen.
The routine end-turn income/autosave banner is removed. Autosaving and error
notifications continue. Research, idle-colony and battle prompts still work.

SHIP DESIGNER
Every module takes one slot. All six combat hulls have four offensive slots.
The hull sets module size: frigate/destroyer small (100% output/cost),
cruiser/carrier medium (200%), battleship heavy (300%), Titan heavy (400%).
Four graviton beams therefore have raw volleys of 64/128/192/256 damage on
frigate/cruiser/battleship/Titan, before hit rolls and defenses.

Heavy weapons are powerful against capitals and bases, but track small ships
poorly. Medium and battleship mounts lose 30 percentage points per smaller
target class; Titan mounts lose 35. Tracking can reduce final hit chance to 5%.
Small mounts have no tracking penalty. Tactical hit chances and AI damage
forecasts both use these rules. New AI warships auto-fit researched equipment
from the start and pay the full module cost.

Armor, shield and repair output also scales; weapon range, movement bonuses,
percentage effects and drone counts do not. Carriers can fit at most two bays.
Standard preset ships remain available with their listed costs and ratings.

BALANCE EDITOR AND SAVES
Designer hulls exposes offensive/defensive/support slots, module size, module
scale and tracking penalty. Ship modules contains the small-size base values.
Profile format 1 is upgraded to format 2 without replacing earlier edits.
Save format 37 loads older campaigns. Old custom designs, ships and queued work
retain their previous equipment statistics and capacity rules. Saving an old
blueprint in the designer refits future orders to the new slots and scaling;
existing ships and queued work are preserved.
