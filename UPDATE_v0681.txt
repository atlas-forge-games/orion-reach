Orion Reach v0.68.1 — extract over v0.68, then restart the game.

- Ships now pay maintenance every turn, in orbit and in transit. Both players
  and AI pay, and AI budgets include ships already queued for construction.
- Per ship, per turn: scout/outpost/transport 1 CR; colony ship/frigate 2;
  destroyer 3; mothership 4; cruiser 5; carrier 6; battleship 8; titan 12.
- Custom designs use their hull class's upkeep. Mothership upkeep is additional
  to module costs and still applies during emergency repairs.
- Queued ships are free. Normal completions begin paying next turn; purchased
  ships pay at the next End Turn. Loss, consumption or capture updates upkeep.
- Net income and the Empire budget include fleet costs. Fleets, shipyards,
  production choices, the designer and the encyclopedia show maintenance.
- Buildings, mothership modules and the Ascension Gate can no longer be bought.
  Complete them with Resources and local industry. Buy remains for ships only.

Existing saves are supported. No retroactive upkeep is charged. Save format
49 stays unchanged; balance profile 13 adds editable maintenance under Standard
ships while retaining existing custom balance values.
