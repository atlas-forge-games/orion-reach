Orion Reach v0.66.0 — extract over v0.65.2, then restart the game.

Mothership defenses and artwork:
- Dedicated transparent city-ark illustration throughout the galaxy, system,
  fleet, habitat and combat views. The old drawing remains a missing-file fallback.
- Standard motherships start with 180 hull, defensive lasers (5 damage with
  standard balance) and a 12-point shield generator. Escorts remain useful.

Boarding and spoils:
- Space defeat disables a mothership for three completed turns. Its population,
  economy and facilities survive. Engines, weapons and ship construction are
  unavailable until repairs finish; it returns with at least 10% hull.
- Open an enemy mothership in the system view after defeating its escorts.
  Board and capture: troop transports fight militia and garrison with a +25%
  deck defense bonus. Population and facilities survive capture. Surviving full
  troop squads keep their transports; remaining troops become the garrison.
  Raid stores: a successful boarding action takes credits and resources from
  actual enemy stocks. Ownership remains unchanged. The same home can be raided
  again after ten turns. Varkesh can also take up to a quarter of its population
  as slaves, subject to homeworld capacity and leaving at least one inhabitant.
  Destroy for salvage: an operational armed ship destroys the disabled habitat
  and recovers 10% of its base Resource cost plus installed facility costs.
- Any empire can operate a captured mothership; only Wanderers can build new
  ones. Foreign captures cancel and refund unfinished mothership orders.
- Retreat saves preserve a surviving mothership convoy; disabled homes stay
  behind and do not slow escaping escorts.
- The ground preview shows predicted casualties. Dispatches retain the results.
  AI empires use the same rules and keep their homes out of offensive fleet orders.

Existing campaigns load directly. Save format 47 preserves damage, emergency
repair timers, ownership and raid cooldowns. Balance profile 12 equips previously
unarmed standard motherships while preserving other balance settings. An old
unfinished battle retains its existing shield damage; the generator is charged
at the next engagement. Forgotten hyperlanes stay with their original discoverer.
