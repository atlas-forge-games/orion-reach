Orion Reach v0.66.1 — extract over v0.66.0, then restart the game.

Mothership modules:
- All 19 compatible facilities now have module names aboard motherships.
- Thirteen transparent shipboard illustrations cover all module families.
  Advanced industry, extraction and research use family art with tier markers.
- Names and artwork follow the home through overview, management, production,
  shipyard, empire queue, completion messages and right-click output breakdowns.
- Click installed modules to manage them; click queued modules to inspect or
  cancel them. Click an empty bay to open the construction catalogue.
- Click the Space Hub module to recruit and assign specialists. Manage modules
  also offers Visit Space Hub alongside enable/disable and dismantle controls.
  Recruitment still requires an operational hub and an available visitor.

Costs, output, upkeep, power, research requirements and capacity are unchanged.
Planetary buildings keep their normal names and artwork. Existing motherships,
including captured homes, receive the new presentation automatically. Save
format 47 and balance profile 12 are unchanged; no conversion is required.
