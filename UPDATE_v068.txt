Orion Reach v0.68.0 — extract over v0.67, then restart the game.

New pirate encounters:
- Existing guardians still attack ships entering their systems.
- Pirate guards protect one planet. Other worlds in that system stay accessible.
  Challenge them through System overview → Encounters & search.
- A deliberate two-turn scout search can uncover a pirate flotilla, salvage,
  research archives or no useful discovery. Bring an armed escort. Moving or
  losing the scout pauses progress; a replacement can resume it.
- Rare event raids give three turns of warning, remain for five turns and can
  steal credits once from an undefended settlement. Defeat them for a bounty
  and to recover any stolen credits. Pirates do not capture planets.
- Known pirates have orange map pennants; guarded planets have labels and an
  encounter button. Discoveries, sightings, warnings and rewards appear in News.
- Pirates use the existing tactical combat system and ship artwork, with their
  own flag and color. Fighting them has no diplomatic penalty.
- AI empires use the same searches and combat rules. Search rewards and pirate
  bounties cannot be collected repeatedly.

Settings and saves:
- New-game Pirate encounters defaults to ON; switch it OFF if preferred.
  This controls the new pirate/search features, not the earlier guardians.
- Start a new campaign for these encounters. Older saves load normally with the
  new features disabled, preserving their rules and existing hyperlane network.
- Save format 49 stores searches, hidden sites, pirate damage and active battles.
  Balance profile 12 and existing victory choices are unchanged.

Details: docs/neutral-encounters.md and Info → Combat → Pirates and scout searches.
