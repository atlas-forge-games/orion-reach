AGE OF THE STARS / ORION REACH — v0.81.2 POPULATION DISPLAY

Extract over v0.81.1 into the repository folder containing main.py and orion/.
Replace the included files, then launch normally with python main.py.

Population now appears prominently above colony production, for example:
8 billion (+100 mil / turn) / 16 billion max

Right-click the population line for the growth calculation:
- Food production, consumption, surplus and growth caps.
- Species growth traits and suitability for the current planet type.
- Synthetic assembly and passive growth where applicable.
- Population capacity, progress and time until the next billion.
- Stopped growth, swarm starvation and separate slave populations.

The same controls work aboard mothership habitats. Stored surveys show only
last-observed population; current growth and capacity remain unknown.
Clicking the population line or activating it with the keyboard also opens
the explanation. Close with Esc, another right-click or Close.

The displayed millions are growth progress per turn. The existing simulation
still adds workers in full billions. Growth rules, costs, saves (format 57)
and balance profiles (version 20) are unchanged. No new assets are required.
