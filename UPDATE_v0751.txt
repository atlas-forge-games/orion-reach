ORION REACH v0.75.1 — FULL MOVEMENT FOOTPRINTS

INSTALL
Close the game. Extract this ZIP over your installed v0.75 game, into the
folder containing main.py. Merge folders and replace the included files.
This is an incremental fix and requires the v0.75 update.

FIX
The movement display previously marked only destination anchor cells.
It now highlights every cell covered by a legal destination footprint.
Hovering highlights the exact full landing area, with each square outlined:
2 for destroyers, 4 for cruisers, 6 for battleships/carriers, 9 for titans,
and 12 for motherships.

The highlighted outer cells are clickable. Clicking places the ship exactly
where the hover preview shows. Short moves remain selectable for fast ships
even when their current and destination footprints overlap. Clicking the
current hull still selects it. Movement costs, collision rules and saves use
the existing v0.75 behavior.

VALIDATION
41 targeted tests passed in Python 3.12 with pygame-ce 2.5.8 and dummy SDL
display/audio devices. Checks include real pointer events for five large
ship sizes at 50%, 100%, 200% and 300% zoom, plus existing movement, collision,
selection and tactical action regressions. The rendered 150% titan preview
was visually inspected and is included in screenshots/.

Test command:
PYTHONPATH=tests SDL_VIDEODRIVER=dummy SDL_AUDIODRIVER=dummy PYGAME_HIDE_SUPPORT_PROMPT=1 python3 -m unittest test_combat_zoom test_combat_geometry test_tactical_combat -q

Preview command:
python3 tools/preview_combat_movement.py
