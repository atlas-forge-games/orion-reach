Orion Reach v0.56.0 - Research progression and advanced facilities

INSTALL
Requires the existing v0.55.0 game. This is an incremental update.
Close the game, extract this ZIP into the repository root (the directory
containing main.py), and allow the included files to overwrite older versions.
Restart with python main.py. No additional dependencies are required.

RESEARCH
Physics introduces weapons throughout the tree: pulse at II, ion at III,
quantum at IV, plasma at V, graviton at VI, particle/phased at VII, and heavy
beam/disruptor weapons at VIII. Utility research remains an alternative.
Sensors and fusion torpedoes arrive at II; antimatter torpedoes at VI.
Carriers and flak are alternatives at III, battleships arrive at IV, and
titans compete with bomber drones at VI. All nine branches retain two
exclusive alternatives at every tier and the existing research prices.

NEW FACILITIES
- Robotic factory: Planetary Automation, Computers III.
- Nanofactory: Nanostructured Materials, Materials VII.
- Deep-core mine: Deep Core Drills, Materials III.
- Automated refinery: Automated Refineries, Materials V.
- Advanced research lab: Advanced Laboratories, Physics IV.
- Quantum research lab: Quantum Research Archives, Computers VII.

Each offers stronger fixed output and workforce bonuses, with higher resource
cost, construction work, power consumption and credit upkeep. Each distinct
facility uses its own slot, can be built once per planet and stacks with
other facility types. Advanced facilities do not require a lower-level
facility. Corresponding factory, mine and laboratory research bonuses apply.

The colony's Advanced category lists the new facilities. The construction
screen shows only researched, buildable options. AI development considers
researched facilities while budgeting for upkeep and local power. All six
buildings can be balanced in python editor.py. RESEARCH_TREE.md lists the
complete new default tree and each facility's prices, output and upkeep.

COMPATIBILITY
The revised layout applies to new campaigns using standard balance.
Existing campaigns keep their saved technology positions, choices, costs
and progress. Campaigns from before embedded profiles use their historical
tree. Existing custom profiles also retain their layout and values, while
gaining the six facility definitions automatically. Save format remains 38;
balance profile format is now 3.

If balance.json exists, it remains your chosen custom profile. To adopt all
new defaults for future campaigns, open python editor.py, choose Reset all
and Save. This also resets other custom balance values. Alternatively, edit
only your research tree manually. Existing campaign snapshots are preserved.

VALIDATION
874 regression tests passed, including new facility output, power priorities,
research gates, duplicate prevention, AI choices, editor changes and old-save
compatibility. Research and colony screens were rendered and reviewed.
