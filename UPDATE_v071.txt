Orion Reach v0.71.0 — Fleet cooperation and combat ship sizes

Requires the complete v0.70.0 installation. Extract this update into that
folder, accepting replacement of matching files. Do not delete other files.
Dependencies are unchanged. Launch the campaign with python main.py.

Try the new scenario with:
    python combat_simulator.py --preset 7

Features
- Passive destroyer escort arrays protect nearby allies from projectiles
  and drones. Beams bypass interception; shared protection is capped.
- Cruiser command relays and titan flag bridges improve nearby ships' aim.
- Cruiser ECM projectors protect nearby allies by reducing incoming accuracy.
- Support uses normal equipment slots and costs; no new support actions and
  no selectable battle plans. Individual movement and targeting are retained.
- Standard destroyers and titans get their respective stock support roles.
  Custom ships require fitted support equipment. Existing custom ships keep
  their loadouts; updating a blueprint changes only future campaign builds.
- Coverage circles, support links, defense/aim readouts and defensive bursts.
- Different combat sprite sizes and class-scaled explosions, while every
  ship keeps one grid cell and the same clickable area.
- Support-aware tactical AI and affordable mixed-fleet production.
- Combined arms simulator preset, equipment help and documentation.

Compatibility
Campaign save format: 50 (unchanged).
Balance profile: 15 (automatically upgrades older profiles, retaining edits).
Combat scenario format: 1 (unchanged).
Support is derived from current positions and survivors, including on load.
Guaranteed-hit simulator mode disables command/ECM accuracy effects, while
escort interception remains active. Old scenarios can have different outcomes
under the new combat rules and AI.

See docs/fleet-support.md and TEST_REPORT_v071.txt for details.
