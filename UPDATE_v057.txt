ORION REACH v0.57.0 — SPACE HUBS AND UNIQUE SPECIALISTS

INSTALL
Extract this incremental update into your existing v0.56 Orion Reach project,
replacing the included files. Keep assets, saves and your custom balance.json.
Run python main.py. No additional game dependencies are required.

GET STARTED
1. Queue a Space Hub on an owned colony. It costs 60 Resources and 36 work,
   needs 2 energy and costs 2 credits per turn with standard balance.
2. After it is operational, advance a turn. The first Hub offers a leader and
   explorer if the shared character pool has them available.
3. Click the Hub on the planet, the planet's Specialists button, or
   Empire > Specialists. Review each visitor's skills, fee and salary.
4. Hire, then choose a colony for a leader or a system for an explorer.
   Hired and Discoveries tabs let you change assignments and inspect progress.

STANDARD RULES
- 18 distinct characters globally: 10 colony leaders and 8 explorers.
- Each has a unique name, biography and starting skill combination.
- No duplicates across empires. Characters retain XP across employers.
- Hiring fees: 55–90 CR. Salaries: 2–4 CR per hired turn, including reserve.
- Four hires and three simultaneous visitors per empire; one leader per colony
  and one explorer per system. Several leaders may serve different colonies.
- First Hub introductions are followed by one arrival check every four turns.
  Terran/Concord: 80%; Aelari/Lithar/Kharuun: 55%;
  Remnant/Nexus/Varkesh: 30%. Other races use 55%.
- Visitors stay six turns and cannot visit two empires at once. Extra Hubs add
  meeting locations, not extra arrival rolls. Shutdown, power loss or loss of
  the host colony makes unhired visitors unavailable; existing hires stay.
- One XP per hired turn, including reserve. Total XP for levels 2–5:
  5, 15, 30, 50. Each level adds one to every starting skill. Missing skills
  stay zero. New levels apply after that turn's work. Pay stays fixed.
- Leaders add fixed local output before applicable empire modifiers. Food
  skills do not create a food requirement for civilizations that need none.
- Explorers need a colony in a system without foreign colonies. A foreign
  fleet pauses their work, but salary and XP continue. Assignments take
  effect immediately; specialist travel time is outside this first version.
- Each system has one finite expedition site, separate from ordinary scout
  discoveries and black-hole relics. Surveying takes 12 work. Recovery needs
  the site's skill at level 2 or better:
    Sealed trading station: Hacking, 18 work, 120 CR.
    Rare mineral asteroid: Salvaging, 20 work, 80 Resources.
    Ancient observatory: Archaeology, 18 work, 80 RP.
- Recovery uses the relevant skill as work per turn. Insufficient skill blocks
  recovery; switch explorers or gain levels. Progress survives reassignment
  and conquest; completing the site pays once and returns its explorer to
  reserve. Losing an assignment returns staff to reserve; an eliminated
  empire releases its staff back to the shared pool after a cooldown.
- Salaries are charged through normal net income, once per turn. As with
  building upkeep, insufficient income can put the treasury into debt.
  Dismissal stops pay immediately and does not refund the hiring fee.
- The AI builds a Hub after basic industry and food are established, reserves
  its first expansion before investing in recruitment (Varkesh are exempt), hires
  within its budget and uses the same assignments and finite sites.
- Visitor arrivals, hires, level gains, discoveries and rewards appear in
  Turn news; private rival activity is not exposed to the player.

BALANCE EDITOR AND SAVES
python editor.py now has Specialists and Expedition sites catalogues plus
Global rules > Specialists. Individual fees, salaries, names, descriptions,
starting skills, role, site requirements/rewards and global limits/rates/XP
can be edited. Names and starting profiles must remain distinct; explorers
need Surveying and characters must use skills appropriate to their role.

Existing saves keep their research layout, economic values and balance.
Older profiles gain only the new definitions/settings. Existing campaigns
start with no hires or visits; build a Hub to begin. This release writes save
version 39 and balance profile version 4. Earlier executables cannot load
newly written saves. Loading an older save never rewrites its source file.

This first version includes leaders and explorers. Fleet admirals, espionage,
counterespionage and offensive bounty-hunter missions are not implemented yet.

VALIDATION
All 905 automated tests pass, including 31 specialist-specific tests.
Automated tests cover fees, pay/debt, local yields and forecasts, XP, unique
visitors, assignments, closed Hubs, conquest, finite rewards, save validation,
old-save/profile migration, AI, private news and real UI controls.
