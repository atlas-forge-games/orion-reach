AGE OF THE STARS / ORION REACH — v0.82 FIXED BUILDING SITES

Extract over v0.81.2 into the repository folder containing main.py and orion/.
Replace the included files, then launch normally with python main.py.

Existing buildings no longer move or shrink when another building is queued,
completed, cancelled or demolished. Headquarters and shipyard retain their
original anchor points. Sites and sprite sizes are stored in saved campaigns.

To choose where to build:
1. Click open ground in your owned colony's planetary overview.
2. Choose a building using Place in the construction menu.
3. Move the preview to the desired spot and click to confirm.
4. Esc or right-click cancels without creating an order or spending resources.

Occupied spots are rejected. Buildings are drawn in front of the landscape,
including trees on the left. The normal construction menu still supports
automatic site selection. Left-click management and right-click building
information continue to work.

Old campaigns load automatically. New saves use format 58; keep all players'
installations updated before sharing those saves. Balance profile version 20,
construction prices, building effects and slot limits are unchanged. This
package does not replace any building or landscape artwork.
