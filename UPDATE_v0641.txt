Orion Reach v0.64.1 — extract over v0.64.0, then restart the game.

Anchored Wanderer motherships are visible and clickable in the solar-system
overview and the galaxy map's system panel. Their inhabitants and habitats count
in the system summary. Opening a home from the system view returns there.

A mothership gives its system the Wanderer empire's ownership color, including
the galaxy name and star ring. Shared systems keep both owners' colors. The
color follows actual anchorage and disappears after departure. Hidden enemy
homes do not reveal their location through ownership labels.

Galaxy mothership badges show the ring silhouette and ship count, with an
arrival countdown during flight. Population is no longer printed on the badge.

A mothership and the ships selected to accompany it have an initial 25 LY range
with standard settings. Onboard reserves add 15 LY to ordinary Wanderer range;
fuel research increases it further. Extra motherships do not stack the bonus.
Scouts and other ships travelling independently keep their ordinary range.
The range overlay, route controls, AI relocation and combat retreats agree.

Existing saves load directly. Save format 45 and balance profile 11 are unchanged.
See docs/wanderer-motherships.md for details. Nothing committed.
