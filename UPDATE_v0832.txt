AGE OF THE STARS / ORION REACH — v0.83.2 FLEET ICONS AND DOCKING

INSTALL
Close the game. Extract this ZIP over v0.83.1 into the folder containing
main.py, orion/ and assets/. Replace the included files, then run python main.py.
This is an incremental update, not a complete game.

IN CLEAN MAP MODE
- A high-quality illustrated ship icon replaces the small geometric symbol.
- A faction-colored rim keeps ownership visible at the compact map size.
- Docked fleets stay at the lower-right of their star/system marker.
- Additional fleets have adjacent slots. Existing docked fleets do not jump
  around when labels change or another fleet arrives or leaves.
- Queued departures remain docked until movement starts.
- Moving fleets appear at their true route position and point toward their
  destination, including diagonals. Arrival returns them to a dock slot.
- Labels and guardian markers make room for the fixed fleet positions.
- The guardian legend is below the map to avoid covering docked icons.

Settings > Interface > Galaxy map style still offers Clean and Classic.
Ship counts and composition remain available by clicking a fleet. Classic's
appearance remains unchanged. No campaign restart, new dependency or save
format change is needed.
